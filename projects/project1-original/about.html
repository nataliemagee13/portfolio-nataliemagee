A little context

I'm Natalie Magee, a Strategic Communications major at TCU's Bob Schieffer College of Communication, minoring in Digital Culture and Data Analytics. I'm graduating early in December 2026 and heading toward a career in PR, brand strategy, and agency work.

I care about brand storytelling, influencer marketing, and building campaigns that feel human instead of corporate. The best work, to me, gives a brand a clear point of view and gives people a reason to care.

Most recently, I worked as an Account/Marketing & Operations Intern at White Unicorn Agency in Dallas. I got to see agency life from the inside: client relationships, research, decks, proposals, and the daily translation of a big idea into useful next steps.

Where I'm headed

I'm looking for opportunities where research, creative thinking, and relationship-building meet. I want to help teams find the story worth telling, then build the strategy that helps it travel.
